TIt has been in the planning for a long time now --- finally is turning into reality. Now heading for the little village of Grebastica Croatia (70 km north of Split) where we have spent 14 previous summer holidays with our kids.  
 Grebastica is a lovely little tourist spot on the Adriatic sea .. but uniquely, no hotels! Just private rooms for hire, lots of local good-will, and a very family friendly atmosphere. 
 That's why we've returned to the same spot so often !
In a few days we will be joined by Ellen and family, Eleanore and family, Emma and Nona, and Nathalie and family. Each with their own living accommodation.  Some coming by car … others by air.  
First to Munich, then to Split and then the beach chairs and sunshade parasoll  by afternoon!!
