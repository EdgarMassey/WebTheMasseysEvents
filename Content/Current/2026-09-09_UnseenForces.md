<span style="font-size: 24px;">Life’s most profound and powerful unseen forces for good in daily life exist quite outside the tangled bonds of religion.<br> Can we call them "spiritual" forces that are accessable to all mankind?
</span><br>


Take something as simple as a <span style="font-size: 24px;">Smile</span> It cannot be measured or weighed, but its impact is undeniable—real, wonderful, and empowering. 


<span style="font-size: 24px;">Simple acknowledgment and goodwill</span>, freely offered to others, carry a quiet but powerful energy for good. 

There is a deeply undervalued strength in <span style="font-size: 24px;"> honest, genuine humility</span> — one that can soften the chaos and conflict of daily life.

<span style="font-size: 24px;">Small acts of kindness</span> have the power to awaken us to new ways of caring for others.

<span style="font-size: 24px;">Generosity</span>, given in unexpected places, can still the storms of self-importance.

<span style="font-size: 24px;">Hospitality</span>, offered to strangers and the overlooked, expands the heart.

<span style="font-size: 24px;">Selflessness </span>can ease the loneliness that hides behind arrogance and self-praise.
