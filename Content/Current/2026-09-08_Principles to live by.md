<span style="font-size: 24px;">Honesty</span><br>
 This principle involves a commitment to truthfulness in all your interactions. It means being sincere and transparent, even when it’s difficult. A recent study showed that 88% of people prioritise honesty in their personal and professional lives, highlighting its universal importance.

<span style="font-size: 24px;">Integrity</span><br>
Living with integrity means adhering to your moral and ethical principles, even when no one is watching. It’s about consistency between your words and your actions.

<span style="font-size: 24px;">Compassion</span><br>
This principle involves showing empathy and concern for others. It encourages kindness, understanding, and a desire to alleviate suffering.

<span style="font-size: 24px;">Respect</span><br>
Treating others with dignity and consideration, regardless of their background or beliefs, is central to this principle. It also extends to self-respect.

<span style="font-size: 24px;">Justice</span><br>
The principle of justice involves fairness and impartiality. It’s about ensuring that everyone receives what they are due and that decisions are made equitably.

<span style="font-size: 24px;">Responsibility</span><br>
Taking accountability for your actions and their consequences is a key aspect of responsibility. It involves fulfilling your duties and commitments.
