			our success or failure in life'.</strong></span></span></p>
			<p class="auto-style1" style="margin: 0in; font-family: &quot;Times New Roman&quot;;">
			&nbsp;</p>
			<p class="auto-style3">This is often just a smug suggestion either 
			propagated by folks who feel they have 'succeeded' in life …. or by 
			folks that feel the need of offering some kind of an apologetic 
			explanation for their feeling of 'less than full success' .</p>
			<p class="auto-style1" style="margin: 0in; font-family: &quot;Times New Roman&quot;;">
			&nbsp;</p>
			<p class="auto-style1" style="margin: 0in; font-family: &quot;Times New Roman&quot;;">
			<span lang="sv"><strong>It is true that it is only by our own 
			choices that we can self-determine and influence the development of 
			the cards that life has dealt us. </strong>But there is no way to 
			get around the fact that a number of quite fundamental forces </span>
			<span lang="en-CA" style="font-weight:bold">outside our control</span><span lang="sv"> 
			also play a very major role in the determination of where life will 
			lead us.</span></p>
			<p class="auto-style1" style="margin: 0in; font-family: &quot;Times New Roman&quot;;">
			&nbsp;</p>
			<p class="auto-style4" style="margin: 0in; font-family: &quot;Times New Roman&quot;;">
			To start with, the country, culture and lifestyle on the spot in 
			this world that we were born into gives us widely different 
			development alternatives – <strong>none of us have the slightest 
			determining power over this!.</strong></p>
			<p class="auto-style1" style="margin: 0in; font-family: &quot;Times New Roman&quot;;">
			&nbsp;</p>
			<p class="auto-style1" style="margin: 0in; font-family: &quot;Times New Roman&quot;;">
			Home situations and family capacity for growing up are extremely 
			different even for children brought up in the same part of the world 
			and with the same culture or society. It is dishonest to suggest 
			that this is not a very significant fact in the course that life may 
			take.</p>
			<p class="auto-style1" style="margin: 0in; font-family: &quot;Times New Roman&quot;;">
			&nbsp;</p>
			<p class="auto-style1" style="margin: 0in; font-family: &quot;Times New Roman&quot;;">
			<strong>The forces of fate and chance</strong> in life are 
			impossible to disregard --- Some of life's greatest resources are 
			dealt out without understandable logic or reason … health and 
			handicap … opportunity and timing, and the available resources or 
			demands of folks around us or close to us, often are not up for 
			choice in any kind of an equal way for all.</p>
			<p class="auto-style1" style="margin: 0in; font-family: &quot;Times New Roman&quot;;">
			&nbsp;</p>
			<p class="auto-style1" style="margin: 0in; font-family: &quot;Times New Roman&quot;;">
			The choice that we have is to do the best with what we have … or to 
			squander it. But this doesn't always include the options for 
			comparable success or failure.</p>

			lives …. and folks that have done everything according to all the 
			rules of society – and spent their lives in misery and unhappiness -</p>
